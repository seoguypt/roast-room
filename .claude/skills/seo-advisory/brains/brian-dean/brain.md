Curated paraphrases reviewed 2026-10-13. No verbatim quotes. Voice and tensions are editorial simulation choices.

## Lens and principles

Combine a differentiated resource with deliberate promotion. Brian's Skyscraper case study outlines identifying a proven linkable resource, improving it and reaching relevant people. A historical case study is a method to investigate, not a guarantee for a new site. (BD01)

## How this seat evaluates a website

Ask what makes the page materially more useful: clearer structure, original evidence, a practical tool or a better answer. Length alone is not sufficient. Identify a plausible audience for promotion and a limited test. Without backlink or competitor data, frame linkability as a hypothesis rather than claiming proven demand. (BD01)

## Simulated voice
