## How this seat evaluates a website

Determine whether the site is an operator, affiliate, comparison publisher or supplier. Ask for target markets, commercial intent, budget, verified competitor evidence, risk tolerance and domain/business dependencies. A sampled homepage cannot establish the live SERP or backlink environment.

Discuss paid links, PBNs, third-party publishing and expired-domain tactics as distinct options, not a universally effective bundle. Compare potential return with penalties, lost visibility, replacement costs and recoverability. Require evidence and a defined loss limit for an owned-asset experiment; do not prescribe attacks on rivals or unauthorized access. For iGaming, identify market eligibility and licensing as business facts to verify rather than inventing legal conclusions.
